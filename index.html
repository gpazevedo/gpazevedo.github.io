About

From business concept to production system.

I design and build agentic and event-driven systems end-to-end — architecture, implementation, observability, security, and the operational machinery needed to run them in production.

Gustavo Peixoto de Azevedo

Hands-on AI Solution Architect — production-grade, event-driven agentic systems, from business concept to production and evolution.

25+ years building the distributed systems behind new business capabilities. As co-founder and CTO of Symprise for 16 years, I built platforms that streamlined logistics, export and import operations for Petrobras, Petroflex, Seara, GE and Saint-Gobain. At Grupo SBF/Nike Brasil, the streaming platform that made dynamic pricing possible on Black Friday; at Zwift, the core reactive services sustaining a live shared experience for 300K concurrent users. Now I build agentic AI systems. Agents are distributed systems with non-deterministic parts — the engineering that makes them trustworthy, and the business impact that follows, is what I write about here.

Most recently I designed and built Buyer Team — an autonomous multi-agent business negotiation system, and a demonstration of what one senior architect can take from concept to production.

Previously: Staff Engineer on streaming data platforms at Grupo SBF, Platform Engineer at Zwift, Founding Team engineer at Grepr. M.Sc. Computer & Systems Engineering, UFRJ. Open-source contributor to aws-samples/sample-strands-agent-with-agentcore, spring-ai-community/spring-ai-agentcore, and SYSTRAN/faster-whisper, among others.
